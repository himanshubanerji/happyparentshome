A letter from our founder

Dear friend,

When we opened our doors in 2016, I knew one thing: no elder should spend their last years alone, or lie awake in pain at night with no one to call. After fifteen years in elderly care, I still believe that looking after our elders is not only a family’s duty. It belongs to all of us.

Today our home has thirty beds. Some of our residents have children who visit every week. For others, we are the family they have. Every one of them gets the same nurse at night, the same hot meal, the same seat at our festival table.

We are a small, registered society, and we depend on people like you. Whatever you can give — money, essentials, or simply an afternoon of your time — reaches someone here directly.

With gratitude, Indira Talukdar Founder & Director, Happy Parents Home
